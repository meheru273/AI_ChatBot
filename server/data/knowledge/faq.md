# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I change or cancel an order after placing it?

You can cancel free of charge while the order is still in the **confirmed** state.
Once it moves to **preparing** the food is being cooked and we cannot refund it. To
change an order, cancel and reorder rather than phoning — the kitchen works from the
ticket, so verbal changes get lost at busy times.

## What is your refund policy?

If something is missing, wrong or arrives cold, contact us within 24 hours with your
order number and we will refund that item or the full order depending on the issue.
Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5–10 working days to appear.

## Do you cater for vegans?

Yes. Our house kimchi is made without salted shrimp or fish sauce, which is unusual
and means it is genuinely vegan. Sikhye is also vegan. Tofu Bibim Set, japchae and
tteokbokki are vegetarian, and some can be made vegan on request — ask, because the
default recipes may include egg.

## Is your food halal?

Our beef and chicken are halal certified, and the dishes we label halal are prepared
with them. We do also cook pork on the same premises, so if that matters to you
please phone before ordering. We do not use alcohol in any dish.

## How spicy is the food?

Korean heat tends to be warm and layered rather than sharp. The spiciest things on
our menu are tteokbokki, sundubu stew and the sweet and spicy chicken. If you want
no heat at all, look at bulgogi, japchae, gimbap, the soy-garlic chicken and the
meal sets built on them.

We cannot adjust spice levels on individual dishes — the sauces are made in batches.

## Can I order for a group or an event?

Yes. Orders over £150 or above 20 items are handled as catering. Phone at least 48
hours ahead. We can do platters and can prepare an entirely vegan or entirely halal
spread.

## Do you have a loyalty scheme?

Not currently.

## Do you offer student discounts?

10% off collection orders with a valid student ID, in person only. It does not apply
to delivery or to catering orders.

## Are your prices inclusive of VAT?

Yes, all menu prices include VAT at the prevailing rate.

## Where do you source your ingredients?

Produce is bought daily from New Covent Garden. Korean pantry staples — gochujang,
doenjang, gochugaru, sweet potato noodles — come from a Korean importer in New
Malden. We ferment our own kimchi in-house on a two-week cycle.

## Do you sell gift vouchers?

Not at the moment.

## Can I book a table?

No. We have twelve counter seats, first come, first served. For groups, order for
collection ahead of time so the food is ready when you arrive.
