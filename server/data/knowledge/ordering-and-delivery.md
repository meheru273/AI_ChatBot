# Ordering and Delivery

## Delivery area

We deliver within a 5 km radius of our West Drayton kitchen at 121 Faling Lane,
UB7 8AG. That covers West Drayton, Yiewsley, Cowley, Harmondsworth, Sipson,
Longford, Stockley Park and parts of Hayes and Uxbridge.

If your postcode falls outside the radius the checkout will tell you at the address
step. We do not deliver to Heathrow airside terminals.

## Minimum order and fees

- Minimum order for delivery: **£10.00**, excluding the delivery fee.
- Delivery fee: **£2.99**, flat, anywhere in the radius.
- Collection orders have no minimum and no fee.

There is no separate service charge and no packaging charge.

## Delivery times

Most orders arrive in 35–50 minutes. Fried chicken and stews are cooked to order and
add roughly 10 minutes at peak times. Friday and Saturday evenings between 18:30 and
20:30 are our busiest periods.

Each dish carries a preparation time in our menu database; the quoted time at
checkout is based on the longest-preparing item in your basket plus travel time.

## Scheduling ahead

Orders can be scheduled up to 7 days in advance, in 15-minute slots, for either
delivery or collection. Scheduled orders are prepared to arrive at the slot you
choose, not when you place the order.

## Order tracking

After checkout your order moves through five states: **confirmed**, **preparing**,
**ready**, **out for delivery**, and **delivered**. You can follow it in the order
tracking page, and you will get an email at confirmation and when the order leaves
the kitchen.

## Payment

We take all major debit and credit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay through Stripe.
Payment is taken at the time you place the order, not on delivery. We do not accept
cash at the door.

## Collection

Collection orders are usually ready in 15–20 minutes. Come to the front counter and
give the order number from your confirmation email. We hold collection orders for
30 minutes past the ready time.

## Large and group orders

Orders above £150 or more than 20 items are handled as catering rather than standard
delivery. Please phone at least 48 hours ahead so the kitchen can plan. We can do
platters of gimbap, mandu, japchae and fried chicken for groups, and we can prepare
a fully vegan or fully halal spread on request.
