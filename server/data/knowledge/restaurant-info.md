## The restaurant

Banchan is a Korean grab-and-go kitchen. The format is built around meal sets — a
main, steamed rice and two banchan in one box — designed to be eaten quickly at the
counter or taken away, rather than a long sit-down meal.

We have twelve counter seats. We do not take table reservations; seating is first
come, first served.

## Accessibility

Step-free access from the street, a level interior, and an accessible toilet.
Counter seating is fixed-height. Assistance dogs are welcome.

Our menu is available in large print at the counter on request.
