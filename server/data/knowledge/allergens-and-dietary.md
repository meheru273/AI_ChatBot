# Allergens and Dietary Information

## How we declare allergens

Banchan follows UK Food Information Regulations. Every dish on our menu carries a
declaration for the 14 regulated allergens: celery, cereals containing gluten,
crustaceans, eggs, fish, lupin, milk, molluscs, mustard, peanuts, sesame, soybeans,
sulphur dioxide and sulphites, and tree nuts.

Allergen data for a specific dish is held in our menu database and should always be
checked there rather than inferred from a dish description. If you have an allergy,
ask about the exact dish by name and we will read the declaration back to you.

## Cross-contamination

Our kitchen is small and prepares all dishes in a shared space. We cannot guarantee
any dish is completely free from traces of an allergen, even when that allergen is
not an ingredient. Specifically:

- Fryers are shared between our fried chicken, mandu and haemul pajeon, so gluten
  and crustacean traces are possible in anything fried.
- Sesame oil and toasted sesame seeds are used widely across Korean cooking and are
  present in many dishes as a finishing ingredient.
- Gochujang, doenjang and most of our marinades contain soybeans and wheat.

If you have a severe allergy or anaphylaxis risk, please phone the restaurant
directly before ordering so the kitchen can advise.

## Common allergens in Korean cooking

Soybeans and gluten appear in most Korean savoury dishes because soy sauce
(*ganjang*), fermented soybean paste (*doenjang*) and chilli paste (*gochujang*) are
foundational seasonings. Gochujang is typically made with wheat as well as soy, so
it is not gluten-free.

Sesame appears as both oil and seed, usually added at the end of cooking.

Fish and shellfish derivatives are common in unexpected places. Traditional kimchi
is fermented with salted shrimp (*saeujeot*) or fish sauce, which makes most kimchi
unsuitable for vegans and for people with crustacean or fish allergies. Our house
kimchi is made without seafood so that it can be served as a vegan side.

## Dietary categories we label

**Vegetarian** — contains no meat, poultry, fish or shellfish. May contain egg or
dairy.

**Vegan** — contains no animal products of any kind, including fish sauce and salted
shrimp in fermented items.

**Gluten-free** — contains no cereals containing gluten as an ingredient. Because of
the shared fryer and shared preparation surfaces, we describe these as gluten-free
by recipe rather than certified gluten-free.

**Halal** — prepared with halal-certified meat. Our beef and chicken suppliers are
halal certified. Dishes containing pork are never halal, and we do not serve alcohol
in any dish.

## Ordering with an allergy

The most reliable way to order safely is to tell us which allergens to avoid rather
than asking whether a specific dish is "safe". We can then filter the entire menu
against that exclusion list and return only dishes whose declarations do not include
it. Filtering is done against the structured allergen field on each dish, not by
reading descriptions, so it does not miss anything.
